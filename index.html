Read case study: Improving the DPS change-impacting process
PROJECT PORTFOLIO · PAGE 4

The challenge

Data Platform Services change requests averaged 50 days to assess. Poor-quality supplier responses, incomplete requests and cost-query escalations delayed delivery across multiple teams.

Business Process Analyst · Contract · January 2021 – July 2022

My contribution

  • Mapped the full process from Clarity requests and Jira tracking through architecture review, D&R documentation, walkthroughs, supplier impacting and consolidated responses.
  • Identified bottlenecks with technical business analysts, architects, engagement teams, suppliers and project teams; co-designed and embedded the future process.
  • Trained DPS colleagues to assess incoming requests for clarity, completeness and technical readiness.
  • Facilitated D&R walkthroughs and dependency meetings to identify impactors, risks, assumptions and constraints early.
  • Trained technical business analysts to consolidate supplier responses into coherent impact assessments.

Analysis outputs described

Structured technical requirements, consolidated impact assessments, quality checks and process improvements.

From bottleneck to control

  • Incomplete requests
    Require adequate information; reject unclear or out-of-estate requests.
  • Inconsistent D&R documentation
    Standardise the template, baseline before issue and identify impactors up front.
  • Missed or repeated walkthroughs
    Hold a walkthrough within three days of D&R and agree deliverables before impacting.
  • Late dependencies
    Hold a dependency and scheduling meeting within 5–10 working days of issue; assign issue owners.
  • Missed supplier SLA
    Standardise supplier impact statements of work and enforce the 15-day SLA.
  • Cost and RAID gaps
    Technical BA and POD lead review scope, cost and RAID ownership before returning the assessment.
PORTFOLIO-REPORTED OUTCOME

The result

The project portfolio reports a reduction in average impacting duration from 50 to 27 days within six months, a 25% improvement in project delivery timelines and fewer customer cost queries. These are reported outcomes, not independently audited measurements.

Skills applied

  • Process mapping and bottleneck analysis
    Redesigned the end-to-end impacting process.
  • Process improvement and governance
    Introduced standard templates, QA gates and timed meetings.
  • Technical requirements translation
    Structured change requests into POD-aligned requirements.
  • Supplier and SLA management
    Standardised statements of work and enforced SLA compliance.
  • Impact assessment and cost review
    Checked scope, RAID ownership and cost before customer response.
  • Facilitation and coaching
    Coordinated walkthroughs and trained colleagues in QA and impact consolidation.
